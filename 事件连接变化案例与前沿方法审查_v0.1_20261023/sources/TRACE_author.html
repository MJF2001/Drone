    Hi! Welcome to my homepage. I’m Yaming Ou (欧亚明), an Assistant Researcher at the Tactile Perception and Embodied Manipulation Laboratory, School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), working with Prof. Long Cheng (IEEE Fellow). I received my Ph.D. from the C2DL Lab, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), and UCAS, supervised by Prof. Chao Zhou and Assoc. Prof. Junfeng Fan.

    I was a visiting scholar at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, working with Prof. Howie Choset (IEEE Fellow / AAAS Fellow) in the Biorobotics Lab from March 2025 to March 2026. Previously, I received my B.E. in Robotics Engineering from Southeast University (ranked 2/34; Outstanding Graduate Award).

    My research interests include SLAM, physical intelligence, spatial intelligence, and tactile-based mobile manipulation. My work aims to develop robotic systems endowed with perception, localization, autonomous mobility, and unmanned operation capabilities for extreme real-life applications. Feel free to contact me to discuss robotics and research collaborations.

    IROS 2026

    HybridSplat: Underwater Dense SLAM via Visual-Acoustic Fusion and Physics-Informed 3D Gaussian Splatting

    Combines DVL-constrained metric tracking with physics-informed 3D Gaussian Splatting to jointly estimate scene geometry and underwater attenuation and backscatter.

    Song Xia†, Yaming Ou*†, Junfeng Fan, Chao Zhou, Pengju Zhang, Xiaoyan Liu, Xintao Wang, Xiangyu Sun, Zongyuan Shen. IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2026.

    TMech 2026

    Event-Based Underwater Structured Light System for High-Speed 3-D Reconstruction

    Combines asynchronous event sensing with a refraction-aware scanning-laser model to reconstruct moving underwater objects at up to 500 Hz.

    Junfeng Fan, Yilin Jia, Yaming Ou*, Chao Zhou*, Zengguang Hou. IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (TMech), 2026. [DOI]

    TIM 2026

    Multimodal Fusion for Underwater Localization Through Hierarchical Uncertainty Awareness

    Models uncertainty at measurement, module, and system levels to adaptively fuse visual, inertial, acoustic, and pressure information for underwater localization.

    Yuanbo Xue†, Yaming Ou†, Junfeng Fan*, Chao Zhou, Bing Wang*, Chih-Yung Wen. IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement (TIM), 2026. [DOI] [Code]

    TMech 2025

    PL-VAP: A Tightly Coupled Self-Localization Framework for Underwater Robots Using Point-Line Features and Visual-Acoustic-Pressure Sensor Fusion

    Jointly optimizes point and line features with DVL motion and pressure depth constraints to maintain reliable underwater localization in sparsely textured scenes.

    Yaming Ou, Song Xia, Junfeng Fan*, Chao Zhou, Yupei Huang, Penghang Shuai, Yuanbo Xue, Zengguang Hou. IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (TMech), 2025. [DOI] [Animation]

    TIE 2025

    Hybrid-VINS: Underwater Tightly Coupled Hybrid Visual Inertial Dense SLAM for AUV

    Uses active structured-light depth to strengthen passive visual-inertial initialization, feature tracking, and loop closure while producing dense underwater maps.

    Yaming Ou, Junfeng Fan*, Chao Zhou, Pengju Zhang, Zeng-Guang Hou. IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics (TIE), 2025. [DOI] [Details] [Video]

    TSMC 2025

    Structured Light-Based Underwater Collision-Free Navigation and Dense Mapping System for Refined Exploration in Unknown Dark Environments

    Integrates scanning binocular structured light, multirate sensor fusion, and air-water extrinsic calibration to enable simultaneous obstacle avoidance and dense mapping in dark water.

    Yaming Ou, Junfeng Fan*, Chao Zhou*, Song Kang, Zhuoliang Zhang, Zeng-Guang Hou, Min Tan. IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems (TSMC), 2025. [DOI] [Paper] [Video]

    Compensates scan distortion using inertial measurements and a velocity model, then registers point clouds to reconstruct dense underwater geometry while the robot moves.
